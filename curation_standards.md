# HiddenFoodGem.com: Internal Back-End Curation & Ingestion Standards

Every restaurant, kitchen, or food-hall maker must pass all 6 internal quality standards before being approved and published to the directory.

---

## 🛡️ The 6-Pillar Internal Quality Filter

### 1. The Food & Craft (Signature Destination Dish)
- **Independent / Family-Operated:** Zero national corporate fast-food chains or franchises.
- **Scratch-Made Craft:** The kitchen must have a passionate culinary identity with at least one verified signature dish people travel for (e.g. slow-smoked Texas brisket, handmade birria, scratch-made pasta, hand-pulled noodles, artisan sourdough/pastries).

### 2. Clean, Safe & Vibrant Ambience
- **Setting Type:** Can be a standalone brick-and-mortar restaurant, an artisan food-hall stall, a modern café, or a cantina.
- **Environment Standard:** Must offer clean, well-lit, comfortable seating (spacious dining room, lively outdoor patio, or vibrant food hall).
- **Family & Friend Safety:** Located in a safe, accessible, well-kept commercial center, walkable downtown corridor, or culinary district where parents feel completely comfortable bringing their children.

### 3. Active Social & Review Proof (Mandatory for Featured Spot)
- **Active Social Media is Required to be Featured:** The restaurant **must** have an active, verified presence on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or active online community reels/tags with up-to-date food photos and customer engagement. **If a restaurant does not have any active social media presence, it CANNOT be featured.**
- **High Customer Sentiment:** Minimum 4.3+ to 4.8+ rating threshold with consistent praise from real local diners regarding food quality, warm service, and kitchen cleanliness across Yelp and Google.

### 4. Visual Proof of Product
- **Real, Authentic Food Presentation:** Verified, appetizing real customer and house photography of actual dishes (zero stock images, zero generic illustrations).

### 5. Architectural & Exterior Curb Appeal (Visual Ambience Verification)
- **Storefront & Exterior Aesthetics:** The exterior of the restaurant, building facade, signage, entrance, and surrounding plaza/center must look clean, attractive, well-maintained, and inviting from the street.
- **Interior & Seating Aesthetics:** Photos of the dining room, patio, order counter, or food-hall seating must show great lighting, clean tables, appealing decor, and a welcoming visual atmosphere. If a place looks run-down, dingy, or neglected from the outside or inside, it is disqualified.

### 6. Strict Exclusion for Negative Reviews & Red Flags (The Integrity Guard)
- **Zero Tolerance for Chronic Red Flags:** If recent or recurring reviews report issues with **cleanliness, bugs/pests, dirty tables/bathrooms, food poisoning/stale food, rude or hostile management, or gross order errors**, the restaurant is **immediately disqualified and rejected**.
- **Review Sentiment Audit:** We actively scan recent 1-star and 2-star reviews across Google, Yelp, and community boards. A high average score is not enough if recent diners warn about unhygienic conditions or declining food quality. Only spots with a consistent track record of pride, cleanliness, and hospitality make the cut.
