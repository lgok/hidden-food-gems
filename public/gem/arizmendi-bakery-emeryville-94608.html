Arizmendi Bakery & Pizzeria (Emeryville Worker-Owned Cooperative)

Independent East Bay Worker-Owned Cooperative Sourdough Bakery & Pizzeria (San Pablo Ave) — Emeryville, CA 94608

Beloved East Bay worker-owned cooperative bakery and pizzeria at 43rd and San Pablo in Emeryville, famous for its daily rotating organic sourdough pizza, warm scones, brioche knots, and crusty hearth loaves.

Named in honor of cooperative movement founder José María Arizmendiarrieta, Arizmendi Bakery brings East Bay families together over honest organic flour, long-fermented sourdough, and community spirit.

👨‍🍳 Why Locals Love Arizmendi Bakery & Pizzeria (Emeryville Worker-Owned Cooperative)

Family & Culinary Roots: Sister cooperative to Berkeley's historic Cheese Board Collective, Arizmendi Bakery at 4301 San Pablo Avenue in Emeryville is a 100% independent worker-owned bakery where every baker has an equal voice and families line up for warm morning scones, sourdough loaves, and daily gourmet vegetarian pizzas.

Scratch Kitchen Commitment: Organic naturally leavened sourdough breads (Walnut Sourdough, Fig-Fennel, Multi-Seed), scratch-laminated sourdough croissants, fresh berry scones, and daily-changing thin-crust sourdough pizzas baked hot all day.
