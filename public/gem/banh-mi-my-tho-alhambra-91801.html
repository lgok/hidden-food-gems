Bánh Mì Mỹ Tho

Legendary Cash-Only Saigon-Style Bánh Mì & Cơm Tấm Counter (Alhambra / SGV) — Alhambra, CA 91801

Widely revered as one of Southern California's greatest Vietnamese sandwich counters—cash-only, zero frills, and famous for its feather-light crispy baguettes and rich homemade egg-yolk butter spread.

Long before artisanal sandwich shops became trendy, Bánh Mì Mỹ Tho perfected the art of the Saigon street baguette on Valley Blvd in Alhambra.

👨‍🍳 Why Locals Love Bánh Mì Mỹ Tho

Family & Culinary Roots: An iconic, family-run San Gabriel Valley institution on Valley Boulevard where generations of locals line up starting at 7:00 AM for shatteringly crisp baguettes and scratch-whipped egg-yolk butter mayonnaise.

Scratch Kitchen Commitment: House-whipped rich golden egg-yolk mayonnaise (bơ), slow-simmered pork meatballs (xíu mại), charbroiled lemongrass pork (thịt nướng), flaky pâté chaud pastries, and strong phin-dripped cà phê sữa đá.
