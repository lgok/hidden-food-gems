Butter & Crumble (North Beach San Francisco Viennoiserie & Espresso)

Woman-Owned Cult-Favorite Laminated Pastry & Espresso Bakery (North Beach, SF) — San Francisco, CA 94133

Located at 271 Francisco St in San Francisco’s North Beach (94133)! Butter & Crumble (@butter.and.crumble) is chef Sophie Smith’s acclaimed small-batch viennoiserie and espresso bar, drawing neighborhood lines for its rotating monthly laminated pastries, brown-butter cinnamon crumble rolls, savory Morty P croissants, and specialty coffee.

Every month brings a brand-new seasonal menu compilation—100% independent, scratch-laminated daily, and paired with local specialty espresso.
