Cafe Momoka

Artisanal Japanese Sandos & Ceremonial Matcha (Ontario Ranch) — Ontario, CA 91761

An aesthetic Japanese-inspired cafe in Ontario Ranch renowned for fluffy shokupan milk bread sandwiches—notably their signature creamy egg sando with soft-boiled egg—alongside strawberries and cream sandos, artisanal matcha, and creative cold brews.

Cafe Momoka brings Tokyo kissaten cafe culture to the Inland Empire. Every sandwich is assembled fresh on delicate shokupan milk bread, paired with hand-whisked matcha and sparkling fruit refreshes in a serene, family-friendly setting.
