Cannataro's Italian Restaurant

Calabrian Family Recipes & Wood-Fired Baked Pastas (Chino Landmark) — Chino, CA 91710

A cherished Chino culinary landmark since 2005. Renowned across the Chino Valley for bubbling baked lasagna, sizzling chicken parmigiana, crispy garlic rolls, and hearty Italian family favorites in an inviting, family-focused dining room.

Cannataro's Italian Restaurant brings genuine southern Italian comfort to Mountain Avenue in Chino. Every batch of marinara and bolognese is simmered low and slow from Italian plum tomatoes and fresh sweet basil.
