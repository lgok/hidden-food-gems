Cinnapop Cafe — Handcrafted Cinnamon Rolls & Matcha (Soft Opening)

Sacramento Farmers Market Pop-Up Turned Brick-and-Mortar Bakery — Sacramento, CA 95814

Founded by husband-and-wife team Tola Vong and Chhayleang Tang after building a devoted following at Sacramento farmers markets, Cinnapop Cafe brings freshly baked gourmet cinnamon rolls, ceremonial matcha drinks, and soft-serve ice cream to Midtown Sacramento (611 16th St).

After 15 years perfecting scratch baking and winning over Sacramento farmers markets as a beloved pop-up, Tola Vong and Chhayleang Tang officially opened the doors to their first brick-and-mortar storefront in Midtown on November 13.
