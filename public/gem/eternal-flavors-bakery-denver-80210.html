Eternal Flavors Bakery (South Broadway Denver)

Award-Winning Mexican-American Family Cake & Pastry House (Denver Platt Park) — Denver, CO 80210

Voted Westword's Best Bakery for Cakes! Now open in their new South Broadway home (1886 S. Broadway, Denver), Melchor Ocampo and family bake Denver's legendary Spring Fling Cake by the slice, Mango Cream Cakes, and craft lattes from scratch.

When The Market at Larimer Square closed after 40 years, Denver locals thought the famous Spring Fling cake was gone forever—until its original baker Melchor Ocampo opened Eternal Flavors Bakery with his family.

👨‍🍳 Why Locals Love Eternal Flavors Bakery (South Broadway Denver)

Family & Culinary Roots: A proud Mexican-owned family bakery helmed by master baker Melchor Ocampo (who spent decades crafting cakes at Denver's historic Market at Larimer Square) alongside his family.

Scratch Kitchen Commitment: Strictly small-batch family baking—never bulk or factory-made. Famous for keeping Denver's iconic zucchini-based Spring Fling cake alive both whole and by the slice.
