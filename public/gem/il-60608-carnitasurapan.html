👨‍🍳 Why Locals Love Carnitas Uruapan

Family & Culinary Roots: Historic Pilsen institution founded in 1975 by Inocencio Carbajal, simmering Michoacan carnitas in giant copper cauldrons for 50 years.

Scratch Kitchen Commitment: Pork simmered slow in rendered lard with orange peel and Mexican spices in copper cazo kettles; served by the pound with fresh masa tortillas.
