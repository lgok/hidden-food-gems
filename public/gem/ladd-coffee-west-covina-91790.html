LADD Coffee (West Covina Korean Cream-Top Coffee & Brioche Donuts)

Independent Korean Specialty Cream-Coffee & Small-Batch Donut Bar (West Covina, SGV) — West Covina, CA 91790

Located at 987 S. Glendora Ave, Unit 3 in West Covina (91790)!
