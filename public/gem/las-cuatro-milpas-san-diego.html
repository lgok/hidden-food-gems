👨‍🍳 Why Locals Love Las Cuatro Milpas (Barrio Logan)

Family & Culinary Roots: Founded in 1933 by Petra and Nati Estudillo, operated continuously by three generations of Estudillo family women under the Coronado Bridge.

Scratch Kitchen Commitment: Heirloom corn boiled and ground in-house daily for hot handmade corn tortillas; deep-fried rolled tacos topped with crumbled cotija and fresh crema.
