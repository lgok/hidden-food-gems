Spirit of Texas Craft BBQ (Norco)

Authentic Central Texas Offset Smoked BBQ (Horsetown USA) — Norco, CA 92860

Norco's premier craft smokehouse. Specializing in authentic Central Texas offset-smoked Prime brisket, St. Louis ribs, jalapeño cheddar sausages, and down-home country sides in an alcohol-free, family-oriented smokehouse setting.

Spirit of Texas brings the legendary smokehouse traditions of Lockhart and Austin straight to Hamner Avenue in Norco. Every cut of beef and pork is seasoned by hand and tended by fire for up to 14 hours.
