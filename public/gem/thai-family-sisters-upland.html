Thai Family Sisters Restaurant

Chiang Mai Khao Soi & Wok-Fired Siamese Homestyle — Upland, CA 91786

Skip generic sweet-syrup Thai—Thai Family Sisters simmers real Northern Chiang Mai Khao Soi and chars flat rice noodles in a screaming-hot wok.

Tucked inside Upland Village just north of the I-10 up Mountain Ave, the sisters greet regulars by name and adjust spice levels with fresh pounded bird's-eye chiles.
