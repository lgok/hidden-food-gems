👨‍🍳 Why Locals Love Tio's Tacos (Historic Riverside)

Family & Culinary Roots: Founded in 1990 by Martin Sanchez and his family, turning authentic Michoacán recipes and recycled visionary sculptures into one of California's most famous culinary landmarks.

Scratch Kitchen Commitment: Slow-simmered Michoacán pork carnitas in copper kettles, scratch-pressed sopes, freshly made fruit aguas frescas, and tender carne asada.
