Vince's Spaghetti (Original 1945 Landmark)

1945 Original Grandma Rose's Mushroom-Beef Ragu & French Dip — Ontario, CA 91762

Operating on the exact same Ontario block since September 1945, Vince's is a multi-generational family institution famous for its rich meat sauce and 1940s French Dip.

Before spaghetti took over, Vince's opened in 1945 selling French Dip sandwiches on the old Highway 70-99. Eighty-one years later, the same family still runs the kettles.
